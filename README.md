# Demand Forecasting & Inventory Optimization

Portfolio project using Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI to forecast SKU demand and convert forecast uncertainty into inventory policies.

## Dataset
- 24 synthetic SKUs
- 4 categories
- Weekly demand from Jan 2024 to Sep 2026
- Promotion flags, lead times, unit costs, ordering costs, holding rates, target service levels
- Mix of regular and intermittent demand

## Analysis
1. Demand profiling and data quality
2. Forecast benchmark comparison
3. MAE, RMSE, and MAPE
4. ABC/XYZ segmentation
5. EOQ, safety stock, and reorder point
6. Inventory cost/service-level trade-offs
7. Power BI dashboard

> Synthetic data for portfolio use.
